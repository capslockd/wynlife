What to Expect

What a Sunday Is Like

Our Sunday gathering is a friendly time of worship for all ages and many cultural backgrounds. It is warm, welcoming, and not too formal.

🎵 We sing together
Our worship team leads contemporary songs of praise.
📖 We open the Bible
The preaching works through Scripture and connects it to everyday life.
🙏 We pray for one another
We make space to pray together and to hear from God.
☕ Morning tea after
Stay after the service for morning tea and a chat, if you'd like to.

Our pastors would love to meet you. Feel free to say hello after the service.

Bringing Children

Sunday School

Sunday School runs during the service for children of all ages. Children join the service with you for praise and worship, then go to Sunday School.

You sign your child in, and you choose a four-digit PIN as you do. You give that same PIN back when you collect them, so your child is only ever signed out to you.

Lessons follow the Generations of Grace curriculum, which walks through the story of the Bible from Genesis to Revelation.

More About Sunday School
Once a Month

Stay for Lunch

On the fourth Sunday of each month we share a Fellowship Lunch after the service. You are welcome to stay. If you can, bring a plate of food to share.

Fellowship Lunch Details
One Less Thing to Wonder About

You Are Not Expected to Give

If you are visiting, giving is not for you to think about. There is a free-will offering that supports the work of the church, and taking part is entirely your choice. Nothing is required of you.

Watch a Service First
