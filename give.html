Giving at WynLife

Giving is an act of worship. We give because God first gave to us — not out of pressure or obligation, but out of joy and gratitude for all He has done.

“Each one must give as he has decided in his heart, not reluctantly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver.” — 2 Corinthians 9:7

Your giving supports the day-to-day ministry of WynLife Church, our Food Bank and community outreach, and the missionaries and mission work we partner with.

How to Give

Electronic Giving

The simplest way to give is by direct bank transfer from your online banking or banking app. You can give a one-off gift or set up a regular scheduled transfer.

Please add a description to your transfer so we can direct your gift to the right place — see what to write below.

What to Write on Your Transfer

When you make your transfer, use the description or reference field to tell us what your gift is for.

01
Offerings

02

Gifts directed to the missionaries and mission partners WynLife supports at home and overseas.
03
Other
Anything else — for example the Food Bank or a specific appeal. Just name it in the description and we'll direct it accordingly.
