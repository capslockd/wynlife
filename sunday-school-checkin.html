Sunday School Check-In

Parents & Guardians

Sign your children in & out

Search for your family, tick In to sign a child into Sunday School, and tick Out when you collect them. The Out box only becomes available once a child has been signed in.

Signing in gives you a 4-digit collection PIN. Keep it with you — you will need it to sign your children back out, so that nobody else can collect them.

Can't find your child? Please see one of the Sunday School team, or email us.
